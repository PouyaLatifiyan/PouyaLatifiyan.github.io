Signal processing

When does a signal become a warning?

I’m Pouya Latifiyan, a doctoral researcher at Beihang University. I study how to detect a change in a dynamical system and whether the warning comes in time to act.

Current research

Alert

A Unified Mathematical Framework for Evaluating Detection Reliability, Alert Timeliness, and Safety in Dynamical Systems

In the Alert project, I connect three questions. Can a detector distinguish a change from ordinary variation? How much time is left when it raises an alert? What happens to the physical system if it stays silent?

Potential applicationsDeep-space navigation · Autonomous vehicles · UAVs · Surgical robots

Future directions

Designing new AI systems

My next goal is to design original AI systems, starting from the mathematical questions raised by detection and timely decisions.

Quantum navigation & computing

I plan to explore quantum formulations for navigation and investigate how to implement them on quantum computers.
