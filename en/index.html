Current research

Alert

A Unified Mathematical Framework for Evaluating Detection Reliability, Alert Timeliness, and Safety in Dynamical Systems

In the Alert project, I connect three questions. Can a detector distinguish a change from ordinary variation? How much time is left when it raises an alert? What happens to the physical system if it stays silent?

Potential applicationsDeep-space navigation · Autonomous vehicles · UAVs · Surgical robots
