Biography

From aviation to signal processing

My path began in Tehran with electrical engineering and aviation telecommunications. At Beihang I turned to spacecraft navigation, studying how a single image of star trails can reveal a spacecraft’s angular velocity. Today my doctoral research brings me back to signals, and to the decisions we make from them.
