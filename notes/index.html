Signal processing

Why alert timing matters

Imagine a vehicle that needs two seconds to brake. If it will reach an obstacle in ten seconds, its last opportunity to start braking is at eight seconds. Raising a detector’s threshold can delay its first alert beyond that deadline. Estimating reliability would require many trajectories and a model of how they arise.
